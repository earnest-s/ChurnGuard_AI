Problem & dataset
Predict whether a telecom customer will churn (binary classification; churn is the positive class). The IBM Telco sample has 7,043 accounts and
20 columns. After dropping customerID and 11 rows with a blank TotalCharges, the cleaned data has 7,032 rows and 19 features (3
numeric, 16 categorical) with a churn rate of 0.266. The split is stratified 80/20: 5,625 train / 1,407 hold-out rows (random_state=42). F1 is
the primary metric because the classes are imbalanced and a missed churner costs more than a wasted retention offer; precision, recall and
ROC-AUC are reported alongside it.
Step 2 · Optuna Bayesian optimisation (baseline)
Optuna's TPE sampler tuned regularisation C, class_weight and fit_intercept with 3-fold stratified CV on the training partition only. Best CV F1 0.6338;
hold-out F1 0.6061 (precision 0.4901, recall 0.7941, ROC-AUC 0.8351). The untuned reference scored 0.5960 CV / 0.6099 hold-out.
Step 3 · PyCaret vs FLAML under the same 60-second budget
PyCaret's internal split is disabled (train_size=0.999) and compare_models is capped at 60 seconds; FLAML is capped at time_budget=60 seconds.
Both are scored on the exact same 1,407-row hold-out, so the comparison is apples-to-apples. PyCaret selected logistic regression (0.6082); FLAML spent its
budget on a small random forest (0.5844).
Step 4 · Critical evaluation: deliberate leakage
A synthetic future_churn_indicator column was appended to copies of the frames as an exact copy of the label. The probe model reached F1 = 1.0000,
which is the expected signature of leakage. The script prints a warning, drops the column, and retrains; the column never enters the real feature frame or the
deployed pipeline. The same audit checks identifier removal, duplicate rows, fit/transform isolation, TotalCharges ~= tenure x MonthlyCharges
redundancy (r = 1.000, known at prediction time, so redundancy rather than target leakage), class balance, and fairness slices.
Global SHAP summary (mean |SHAP|, 200 hold-out rows). Top drivers:
MonthlyCharges 0.1790, tenure 0.1561, InternetService 0.1242.
